# Assumptions (Table 1, CB1-OX1 cells)

- **Data / F1**: I used the `CB1R-OX1R` block of `crc_cAMP_fold.csv` and `crc_barr2_fold.csv`. Each non-empty column is one independent experiment (n = 4 per group), and each value is already the per-experiment mean in fold units. I rejected the alternative of looking for technical replicates because the deposit does not contain them.
- **Mock (0.00 µg) columns (data ambiguity, no methods item)**: the 0.00 µg block has 8 columns and is identical in the OX1 and OX2 sections, so it probably holds two series of mock controls, 4 from the OX1 experiments and 4 from the OX2 experiments. For βarrestin2 I used columns 1-4. Their magnitude (about 4-5 fold at 10 µM) matches the OX1 groups, while columns 5-8 reach about 28 fold, which matches the OX2 groups (30-45 fold). For cAMP I used columns 5-8. They have the same long-float export format as the OX1 0.10/0.25/0.50 columns, while columns 1-4 have the short format and the exact "1" values of the OX2 groups. I rejected two alternatives: pooling all 8 columns, and the opposite assignment. The opposite assignment is printed as a sensitivity run in run.log. It changes the 0.00 row a lot (cAMP pEC50 9.23; βarr2 Emax 29.3, Hill 2.63) and so changes the stars.
- **M3 (exclusion, gap)**: I made no further exclusions. I assumed the deposited fold data already reflect the exclusion of the 3 technical replicates, and there is nothing in the data to identify them. Alternative rejected: guessing an outlier experiment to drop.
- **M4 (Emin normalisation, gap)**: the data are already "fold", so I did not normalise again. Alternative rejected: re-dividing by the lowest-concentration value. That would have changed the fitted values only slightly, and the deposit says it is already normalised.
- **S2 (fit structure, gap)**: I fitted one 4-parameter logistic, Y = Bottom + (Top−Bottom)/(1+10^((LogEC50−X)·Hill)), to each independent experiment separately (6 concentrations, per-experiment means). I then averaged the parameters across experiments. I chose this because S7 (ANOVA + Tukey) needs per-experiment parameter values. Alternative rejected: one fit to all points pooled. Its estimates are printed in run.log as a sensitivity check. They are similar for βarr2, but the cAMP 0.00 and 0.50 µg pooled 4PL fits are ill-determined (huge parameter SEs). Least squares used scipy `curve_fit`, unweighted, with no parameter constraints.
- **S6 (when to constrain Hill, gap)**: I treated a per-experiment 4PL fit as "not accurately estimated" if any of these held: (a) the covariance was not finite; (b) LogEC50 fell outside [−11, −4], i.e. more than 1 log beyond the tested range; (c) |Hill| < 0.3 or > 5; (d) Hill had the wrong sign for the assay; or (e) SE(LogEC50) ≥ 1 log unit. Such experiments were refitted with Hill fixed at 1 in magnitude, and the constrained fit's parameters were used. The rule was applied per experiment, not per group. As a result, the cAMP 0.00 µg group is entirely Hill-fixed, so its Hill = −1 ± 0 (n = 4). The alternative of a group-level rule (constrain all experiments of a group if any fails) was rejected to keep information.
- **S6 sign / Hill sign convention (gap)**: I report Hill slopes in Prism's signed convention, so cAMP inhibition curves have negative slopes and "constrained to 1" for cAMP means −1. Alternative rejected: reporting |Hill|. If the published table prints positive cAMP slopes, take the absolute value.
- **S3 (Emax definition, gap)**: for cAMP, Emax is the fitted plateau at high agonist concentration (the Bottom, fold < 1, the inhibited level). For βarrestin2 it is the fitted Top. Alternatives rejected: span (Top−Bottom), and % inhibition (1−Bottom).
- **S3 (pEC50 "(nM)")**: pEC50 = −LogEC50 (molar), averaged across experiments. The EC50 in parentheses is 10^(−mean pEC50) in nM, i.e. the geometric-mean EC50. Alternative rejected: the arithmetic mean of the per-experiment EC50s.
- **S5 (potency not estimable, gap)**: if even the Hill-fixed fit failed (LogEC50 outside [−11, −4] or non-finite covariance), the code would report Emax as the greatest observed response and exclude pEC50 for that experiment. This never triggered, so all cells are numbers and there are no bounds or "n.d." values.
- **S4 (± meaning, gap)**: ± is the SEM across independent experiments (SD/√n, n = 4). Alternative rejected: the curve-fit standard error of the parameter.
- **S7 (stars, gap)**: for each parameter and assay, I ran a one-way ANOVA across the 4 OX1 groups on the per-experiment fitted values, followed by Tukey HSD (statsmodels). Stars are shown on the transfected group only, for its comparison with 0.00 µg; I read "relative to CP55,940" as the mock-transfected CP55,940 response. Thresholds: *** p<0.001, ** p<0.01, * p<0.05. The legend lists only ** and ***. I kept "*" for p<0.05 rather than silently dropping a computed result. It occurs once (cAMP Hill, 0.25 µg, p = 0.016). Stars are not placed on ± or EC50 cells.
