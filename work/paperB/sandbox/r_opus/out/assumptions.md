# Assumptions

- **S1 (gap: Hill equation)**: Chose a 3-parameter Hill model on log10 concentration, `Rel = Emax / (1 + 10^((logEC50 - logC)·nH))`, with the baseline fixed at 0 % relaxation and Emax, EC50 and nH free (bounds: Emax 0–200 %, EC50 10 pM–1 mM, nH 0.2–5). Why: the curves all start at ~0 % relaxation at 1 nM, and "Hill-type" suggests a free slope. Rejected: a 4-parameter model with a free baseline (it adds a parameter the data do not need), and a slope fixed at 1 for every curve.
- **S1 (gap: fallback for curves without a plateau)**: When the free-slope fit cannot be identified (EC50 above the highest tested concentration of 10 µM, Emax > 100 %, or nH at a bound), the curve is refitted with nH fixed at 1. This happened for 6 of the 120 curves: CSE 1:3 ACh experiments 1 and 2 (free fits gave Emax 39 % / EC50 10 µM and Emax 124 % / EC50 1 mM, against observed maxima of about 18 %), plus 4 DEA/NO or ACh curves whose free Emax was just over 100 %. Why: a free-slope extrapolation that far beyond the data is not meaningful. Rejected: keeping the unconstrained extrapolations. They inflate the CSE 1:3 ACh Emax to about 41 % and its EC50 to about 3.5 µM, which contradicts the data. Also rejected: using the observed maximum response as Emax.
- **S1 (gap: per-experiment vs mean-curve fit)**: Each experiment's curve is fitted separately. Mean ± SEM and the geometric-mean CI are then taken over the 5 fitted values. Why: the caption reports SEM and a CI "from 5 experiments", which needs a value for each experiment. Rejected: fitting the mean curve, which gives no spread across experiments.
- **S2**: Emax is the mean of the 5 fitted Emax values, with SEM = SD(ddof = 1)/√5.
- **S3 (gap: CI)**: The geometric mean is 10^mean(log10 EC50). Its 95 % limits are 10^(mean ± t(0.975, df = 4) · SE) of log10 EC50. EC50 is reported in nM. Why: n = 5 is small, so a t quantile is standard; the log base does not change the result. Rejected: a normal (1.96) quantile.
- **S4 (gap: which test)**: Within each series (CSE, EVE, HTE) and each agonist, the 4 groups (control plus 3 dilutions) are compared by one-way ANOVA, then by Dunnett's test against that series' DMEM control (`scipy.stats.dunnett`). This is done on Emax and on log10 EC50. A star means Dunnett p < 0.05, whatever the omnibus ANOVA p. Stars go on the Emax and EC50 cells only; SEM and CI cells and control rows get "". Rejected: paired t-tests (the caption names ANOVA + Dunnett), and requiring the ANOVA itself to reach p < 0.05 before starring (in this table it changes no star).
