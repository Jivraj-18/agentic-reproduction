# Assumptions (Table 1, CB1-OX1, CP55,940)

- **Data blocks (F1)**: I used the `CB1R - OX1R` block of `crc_cAMP_fold.csv` and the `CB1R-OX1R` block of `crc_barr2_fold.csv`. Each column is one independent experiment, and each value is already a per-experiment mean of the technical triplicate.
- **M2b (n = 4 to 8)**: the 0.00 µg (mock) group uses all 8 columns listed under the "0.00 µg OX1R" header in the deposit, giving n = 8. All other groups have n = 4. Why: the deposit lists these 8 columns under the OX1 header, and this is the only group with 8 columns. The same 8 columns also appear under the OX2 header. Columns 5 to 8 look like mock controls from the OX2 series: in βarrestin2 they reach about 28-fold, against about 4-fold in columns 1 to 4. The alternative I rejected was to use only columns 1 to 4 as the OX1-series mock. With those 4 columns the βarrestin2 0.00 curve fits a clear plateau (Top about 5.1, pEC50 about 6.18). That conflicts with footnote F3, which says this curve did not reach an asymptote.
- **M3 (exclusion)**: no exclusion was applied. The deposit holds only per-experiment means, so the 3 excluded technical replicates cannot be identified or removed. I assume the deposited means already reflect the exclusion.
- **M4 (normalisation / Emin)**: no renormalisation. The data are already in fold (values at 0.1 nM are about 1) and are used as deposited.
- **S2 (fit strategy)**: I fit one curve per condition by unweighted least squares to all per-experiment points pooled (n_exp × 6 points), which is Prism's default for replicate Y columns. I used the Prism variable-slope model and multistart to reach the global minimum. Why: the paper flags only the 0.50 µg cAMP curve as failing the 4-parameter fit, and that is exactly what a pooled fit shows (Hill about -13.5, SEs infinite). Per-experiment fits fail for several curves in different groups. Rejected alternative: fit each experiment separately and average the parameters.
- **S6 / F2 (constrained slope)**: the 4-parameter fit counts as "not accurately estimated" when the parameter SEs are non-finite or the Hill slope is extreme. This happens only for 0.50 µg cAMP, the row the paper marks. That curve is refit with |Hill| = 1. Because the curve falls, the Hill slope is reported in Prism's sign convention as **-1** (the rejected alternative is +1). Its SE is "N/A", and that Hill value is left out of the ANOVA.
- **Hill sign**: all cAMP Hill slopes are reported in Prism convention (Bottom < Top), so they are negative for the falling cAMP curves. Rejected alternative: report absolute values.
- **S3 (Emax for cAMP)**: Emax is the fitted Bottom plateau, i.e. the fold of forskolin cAMP left at maximal inhibition. Rejected alternatives: span (Top - Bottom) and % inhibition. For βarrestin2, Emax is the fitted Top.
- **S5 / F3 (βarrestin2 0.00 µg)**: Emax is the observed value, taken as the mean ± SEM (n = 8) of the responses at the highest concentration (10 µM). For the EC50 bound, I interpolated (linearly in log concentration) the concentration where the mean curve reaches half its observed maximum. Since the true maximum is at least the observed one, the EC50 is at least this value. Result: pEC50 "<5.744" and EC50 ">1.80e3 nM". The Hill slope for this row is still the pooled 4-parameter fit value, because the footnote only replaces Emax and EC50. Rejected alternative for the bound: ">10 µM" (the top concentration).
- **S4 (±)**: ± is the asymptotic standard error of the pooled-fit parameter, as Prism reports it. For the observed βarrestin2 0.00 Emax, ± is the SEM across experiments. Rejected alternative: SEM of per-experiment fitted parameters.
- **S7 (stars)**: one-way ANOVA with Tukey-Kramer, run from summary statistics as Prism allows from mean, SEM and n. The inputs are the fitted value, SD = SE × sqrt(n_exp), and n = number of experiments. Each OX1 group is compared with the 0.00 µg group. Thresholds are * < 0.05, ** < 0.01, *** < 0.001, and "" means not significant. I mark "N/A" where no test was possible: the ± and EC50 cells, the fixed Hill slope, and βarrestin2 pEC50 (the reference group is only a bound, so there is nothing to compare against).
- **EC50 column**: EC50 (nM) = 10^(-pEC50) × 1e9.
